Table for Two

  • Engine / Language Unity 6 • C#
  • Role Lead Programmer & Tech Art
  • Timeline Mar 2024 - May 2026
  • Tags Co-op, Time-Management, Cooking

Overview & Story

In Table For Two, players follow Milo and Miko, two childhood friends who reopen a mysterious restaurant inherited from Miko’s uncle. While managing the restaurant, they discover torn pages from a secret recipe diary, slowly revealing the truth behind the uncle's disappearance and a legendary recipe that powerful figures are searching for.

Gameplay

Each level represents a day in the restaurant, where players must:

  • Prepare recipes and serve customers quickly.
  • Manage multiple complex orders.
  • Improve their kitchen efficiency.

As the game progresses, difficulty increases, new kitchen themes appear, and the story unfolds.

My Contributions

I directed the development of this Unity 6 co-op time-management game, acting as both Lead Programmer and Technical Artist.

  • Programmed core gameplay loops, including complex recipe validation and order management systems.
  • Engineered a dynamic AI behavior tree system for NPCs, enabling state-based actions driven by real-time order status and temporal constraints.
  • Bridged the gap between art and programming by implementing specialized shaders and optimizing the rendering pipeline.

Horizon 86

  • Engine / Language Unity • C#
  • Role Solo Developer & Tech Art
  • Timeline Mar 2024 - May 2026
  • Tags 3D Voxel, Puzzle, Narrative

Overview

Horizon-86 is a narrative-driven 3D exploration and puzzle game prototype that takes the player on a journey through Portugal's historical and technological development. Through interaction and problem-solving mechanics, the game explores the country's transition from joining the EU in 1986 to modernity.

Developed as part of the Video Games program at ETIC Algarve (24/26).
Created for the ERASMUS+ Desafio Criativo 2026 challenge.

Gameplay & Story

In Horizon-86, players control a young explorer in a retro-futuristic setting where time and progress are fragmented. The goal is to overcome physical and temporal barriers to reconnect the country. Each phase of the game represents a decade or a major milestone:

  • 1986: The start of the journey, focused on breaking down walls and opening borders.
  • 1996: Structural reconstruction, symbolized by repairing bridges through resource collection.
  • 2006: Digital Innovation and Economical crisis in Portugal.
  • 2016: The phase of digital innovation and expansion.
  • 2026: The present and the future.

The Visual Style

  • Voxel / Low-Poly aesthetic.
  • Retro-futuristic environment inspired by Portuguese architecture.
  • Stylized character with fluid root-motion animations.

My Contributions

As the Solo Developer and Technical Artist, I was responsible for all aspects of development. This included game design, 3D modeling, texturing, rigging, level design, narrative scripting, and programming all core gameplay mechanics and puzzles.

Jester

  • Engine / Language Unity • C#
  • Event Scream Jam
  • Role PM, UI-UX, Tech-Artist
  • Tags Horror, Puzzle, 2D/3D

Overview

Jester is a 2D/3D horror puzzle game developed during Scream Jam. Players explore a dark, eerie environment while solving puzzles and avoiding the unsettling presence of the Jester, uncovering the story behind the haunted location.

The game combines atmospheric exploration, puzzle-solving, and horror storytelling, creating a tense and immersive experience. Originally made for Scream Game Jam, and now continuing in further development!

How to Play

  • Don’t waste time - search the map for hidden numbers to uncover the exit code!
  • The numbers are scattered across walls and circus balls. Find them, discover the right order, and escape the maze before the Jester finds you.
  • Close the valves around the map to drain the water and keep your speed up.
  • Stay alert… the Jester always knows where you are.

Key Features

  • Horror Atmosphere: Dark corridors, eerie sound design, and suspenseful visuals to create tension.
  • Puzzle Mechanics: Environmental puzzles that must be solved to progress through the game.
  • Exploration & Interaction: Collectible clues and items that reveal the codes and unlock areas.
  • Dynamic Enemy Threat: The Jester chases the player, creating unpredictable challenges and heightening tension.

Littlest Suspets

  • Engine / Language Unity 6 • C#
  • Event Brackeys Game Jam 2026
  • Role AI/Gameplay Prog., Tech Artist
  • Tags Survival Thriller, Deduction

Overview

Cute by the light. Terrifying in the dark.

Welcome to a tense, first-person survival thriller where not everything is as adorable as it seems. You are trapped in a facility with a crowd of innocent, wandering chibi animals... but there are Impostors hiding among them.

When the lights are on, everyone looks exactly the same. But when the facility suffers a blackout, the story quite changes. You have a gun and a limited amount of bullets. Your goal is simple: find and eliminate all the impostors before it's too late. But be careful—shooting an innocent will increase the tension, making the lights flicker more and blackouts more frequent. Kill 3 innocents, and it's Game Over. Fail to kill an impostor during a blackout, and the tension rises. Survive 7 blackouts without a kill, and the impostors will destroy everything.

Can you spot the monsters in disguise before the lights go out?

Created in 1 week in Unity6 for the Brackeys Game Jam 2026! ✨

Key Features

  • Tense Social Deduction: Observe the crowd to spot the impostors. Watch for creepy sounds, suspicious movements, and terrifying glimpses of their true faces.
  • Survive the Blackouts: When the lights go out, the hunters become the hunted. Stay away from the impostors, or they will explode and take you with them.
  • High Stakes: You only have a few bullets, so every shot counts. Shooting innocents will plunge you deeper into the nightmare.
  • Dynamic Tension System: Mistakes and missed opportunities increase the tension, leading to more frequent flickers and longer blackouts.

Controls

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Look around
  • Space: Jump
  • Left Click: Shoot
  • F: Inspect
  • P: Pause

Disclaimer: For the best bugfree game experience, use the Chrome browser! Firefox/Brave/Opera are known to have mouse input bugs in Unity web applications.
